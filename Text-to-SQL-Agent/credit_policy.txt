Financial Institution Credit Risk Division
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 2025

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 1: LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Minimum Eligibility Requirements
All loan applicants must meet the following baseline criteria to be considered for approval:
- Minimum age: 21 years. Maximum age at loan maturity: 70 years.
- Minimum employment duration: 12 consecutive months with current employer.
- Minimum annual income: 60,000 ILS (or equivalent in approved currency).
- Active bank account for at least 6 months.
- No open bankruptcy proceedings.

1.2 Residency and Documentation
Applicants must provide proof of residency, valid government-issued ID, three months of recent pay stubs, and the last two years of tax returns. Self-employed applicants must provide certified financial statements for the last two fiscal years.

SECTION 2: CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 Credit Score Tiers
The institution uses a tiered credit score framework for loan classification:

- Tier 1 — Prime (Score 750–850): Eligible for all loan products. Best available interest rates. No additional collateral required for standard loan amounts.
- Tier 2 — Near-Prime (Score 650–749): Eligible for most loan products. Standard interest rates apply. Loans above 200,000 ILS require one guarantor.
- Tier 3 — Sub-Prime (Score 550–649): Restricted loan products only. Interest rate premium of 2.5% above base rate. Mandatory collateral for all loans. Maximum loan term: 5 years.
- Tier 4 — High Risk (Score below 550): Loan approval requires Credit Committee review. Maximum loan amount: 50,000 ILS. Two guarantors mandatory. Approval rate for this tier is historically below 15%.

2.2 Recent Credit Events
Applicants with any of the following in the past 24 months are automatically escalated to Credit Committee review:
- Loan default on any financial product.
- Three or more missed payments.
- Debt restructuring or settlement agreements.
- Repossession of secured assets.

2.3 Credit Score Monitoring
Approved clients are subject to quarterly credit score monitoring. A drop of more than 80 points from the score at origination triggers a mandatory account review and may result in reclassification to a higher-risk tier.

SECTION 3: DEBT-TO-INCOME RATIO (DTI) POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 DTI Thresholds
The Debt-to-Income ratio is calculated as total monthly debt obligations divided by gross monthly income:

- DTI below 28%: Preferred applicant. Approved automatically if credit score is Tier 1 or Tier 2.
- DTI 28%–36%: Acceptable. Standard approval process applies. Requires income verification.
- DTI 36%–43%: Borderline. Requires Credit Officer review. Approval conditional on strong credit score (above 680) and stable employment exceeding 3 years.
- DTI above 43%: Rejected automatically. No exceptions unless the excess is due to a short-term, documented medical expense, in which case a single-level appeal is permitted.

3.2 DTI Calculation Rules
- Only recurring monthly obligations are included: existing loan payments, credit card minimum payments, rent/mortgage, and court-ordered payments.
- Excluded from DTI: utility bills, groceries, insurance premiums, and voluntary savings contributions.
- For variable income earners (commission-based, freelance), DTI is calculated using the average of the last 24 months of gross income.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 4: EMPLOYMENT AND INCOME VERIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 Employment Stability Standards
Employment duration is a key risk indicator. The following guidelines apply:

- 0–1 years of employment: High risk. Loan applications restricted to amounts below 30,000 ILS.
- 1–3 years of employment: Moderate risk. Standard loan products available with income verification.
- 3–7 years of employment: Low risk. Preferential terms available.
- Over 7 years of employment: Very low risk. Eligible for maximum loan amounts and best available terms.

4.2 Income Sources
Acceptable income sources for DTI calculation:
- Primary employment salary (verified by employer letter and pay stubs).
- Pension income (for retirees aged 60+, verified by pension fund statement).
- Rental income, provided it has been declared for tax purposes for at least 2 years.
- Spousal income may be included only when the spouse is a co-applicant.

4.3 Self-Employment Policy
Self-employed applicants are subject to a 10% income discount applied before DTI calculation, to account for income volatility. Business expenses reported on tax returns are not considered personal income.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 5: RISK CATEGORIES AND LOAN CLASSIFICATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 Portfolio Risk Classification
All active loans are classified quarterly into one of four categories:

- Performing (Green): Loan is current. No missed payments. Default probability below 5%.
- Watch (Yellow): One or two missed payments in the last 12 months, or credit score drop of 50–80 points. Assigned to a relationship manager for monitoring.
- Substandard (Orange): Three or more missed payments, or DTI deterioration beyond 43%. Provisioning of 20% of outstanding balance required.
- Default (Red): Loan is 90+ days overdue. Full provisioning required. Transferred to the Recovery Unit. Legal proceedings may be initiated after 180 days.

5.2 Default Rate Benchmarks
The institution targets a portfolio default rate below 8% on an annual basis. Divisions exceeding 10% default rate for two consecutive quarters are subject to a mandatory portfolio review and lending restriction.

5.3 Concentration Risk
No single borrower may represent more than 2% of the total loan portfolio by outstanding balance. Industry concentration (e.g., real estate, hospitality) is capped at 15% of the total portfolio.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 6: DEFAULT MANAGEMENT AND RECOVERY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1 Early Intervention (Days 1–30 overdue)
Upon first missed payment, the Loan Servicing team initiates contact within 5 business days. A payment arrangement may be offered for up to 3 months without Credit Committee approval.

6.2 Restructuring (Days 31–89 overdue)
Loans overdue between 31 and 89 days may be restructured with the following options:
- Extension of loan term by up to 24 months.
- Temporary reduction of monthly payment (interest-only period up to 6 months).
- Partial write-off, subject to Credit Committee approval and evidence of financial hardship.

6.3 Default and Recovery (Day 90+)
Loans classified as Default are transferred to the Recovery Unit. The Recovery Unit may:
- Initiate legal proceedings to collect outstanding balance.
- Negotiate a settlement for a portion of the outstanding debt (minimum recovery target: 60% of principal).
- Sell the debt to a third-party debt collection agency at a discount.

6.4 Write-Off Policy
Loans are written off the balance sheet after 24 consecutive months in Default status, or when the Recovery Unit determines that collection is not economically viable. Write-offs require CFO approval.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 7: SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.1 Marital Status
Marital status is not used as a standalone approval or rejection criterion. However:
- Married applicants may include spousal income as a co-applicant, which can improve DTI.
- Divorced applicants must disclose court-ordered alimony or child support, which counts as a monthly debt obligation in DTI calculation.
- Widowed applicants: Survivor pension income is treated as stable income if documented.

7.2 Age Considerations
- Applicants under 25: Maximum loan term is limited to 10 years.
- Applicants over 60: Loan term may not extend beyond age 70. Pension income accepted as primary income.
- No loan may be approved if the applicant would be over 75 at the time of final repayment.

7.3 Fair Lending Compliance
All lending decisions must comply with applicable fair lending laws. Gender, ethnicity, religion, and national origin are strictly prohibited as factors in any lending decision. Random audit samples of 5% of all decisions are reviewed quarterly by the Compliance Department.

SECTION 8: LOAN AMOUNT AND TERM LIMITS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.1 Maximum Loan Amounts by Tier
- Tier 1 (Prime): Up to 500,000 ILS unsecured; up to 2,000,000 ILS with collateral.
- Tier 2 (Near-Prime): Up to 200,000 ILS unsecured; up to 800,000 ILS with collateral.
- Tier 3 (Sub-Prime): Up to 80,000 ILS with mandatory collateral.
- Tier 4 (High Risk): Up to 50,000 ILS with two guarantors and Credit Committee approval.

8.2 Maximum Loan Terms
- Personal loans: Up to 7 years.
- Business loans: Up to 10 years.
- Secured real estate loans: Up to 25 years.
- All terms subject to age restrictions in Section 7.2.

8.3 Loan-to-Income (LTI) Limits
The outstanding loan balance may not exceed 5× the applicant's verified annual gross income at origination. For loans above 3× annual income, a second credit officer signature is required for approval.
